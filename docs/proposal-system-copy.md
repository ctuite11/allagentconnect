# Opt-In Agent Proposals — Locked Copy & Positioning (Phase 2)

## Internal Name
**Opt-In Agent Proposals**

Do NOT use:
- leads
- bids
- marketplace
- auctions
- matching service

---

## One-line Global Rule (must appear once in product)
**Proposals are shown only when both sides opt in.**

---

## Agent-facing Core Explanation
**Optional. Permission-based. Client-qualified.**

Agent proposals allow you to make your services and incentives visible **only** to qualified buyers and verified sellers who explicitly opt in.

- No cold outreach
- No auctions
- No obligation

---

## Agent Toggle Microcopy (locked)

### Buyer side toggle
**Show my buyer-agent proposal to qualified buyers**  
Qualified buyers have provided proof of funds, a pre-approval, or agreed to provide documentation upon request.

### Seller side toggle
**Show my listing proposal to verified sellers**  
Verified sellers have submitted property details through AllAgentConnect.

---

## Incentives Language (locked)
**Incentives are optional.**  
If offered, they are shown only to qualified users and may include fee credits, flat-fee options, or reduced commissions.

---

## Buyer-facing Explanation (Phase 2 UI)
**Compare agents — on your terms.**  
Receive proposals only from verified agents who choose to work with buyers like you. No obligation. No spam.

---

## Seller-facing Explanation (Phase 2 UI)
**Choose how you list — before committing.**  
Review services, pricing structures, and incentives from verified agents. You decide if — and when — to engage.

---

## Tone Guidelines
- Calm, premium, and permission-based
- Emphasize control, privacy, and optionality
- Avoid "deal," "bid," "compete," "rank," "winner," "auction"
