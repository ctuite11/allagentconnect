ONE NETWORK. ALL AGENTS.

I built All Agent Connect because, after more than 30 years in real estate, I believe the strength of this business has always been the relationships between agents.

The agents with the strongest networks — the ones who knew what was coming, what buyers were looking for, and who to call — were often the ones best positioned to help their clients.

Technology was supposed to make that easier. Instead, much of our industry has become more fragmented, and more of the information and relationships we create are being controlled by companies outside the transaction.

I believe there should be another way.

A place where agents can connect directly, share buyer demand, off-market and coming-soon opportunities, referrals and information that helps all of us do a better job for our clients.

Not another closed group. Not another network available only to a select few.

That is the idea behind All Agent Connect.

I hope you’ll be part of it.
