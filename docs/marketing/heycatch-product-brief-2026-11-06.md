# All Agent Connect — public website brief (current snapshot)
Updated: November 6, 2026.

This document corrects the outdated facts noted in the Heycatch brief-fit audit. It is an updated summary for public-site work, not an assertion that the original Heycatch input brief was edited.

## Product
All Agent Connect is a private, verified agent-to-agent network, based in Greater Boston and designed for licensed professionals across brokerages. Agents can share permitted information about properties being prepared for sale, buyer and renter needs, referrals, and market intelligence.

## Founder and trust
Founder: Chris Tuite, Boston real estate broker with more than 30 years of experience, inventor of U.S. Patent No. 11,379,939 B2. The public homepage and About page name him. The patent is an independently verifiable public record, not an endorsement of AAC or claim of business results.

## Homepage
Approved brand headline: **ONE NETWORK. ALL AGENTS.** Do not replace it with a market-specific heading without explicit founder approval.
Clarifying category line: a private property-sharing and buyer-demand network for Massachusetts real estate agents.
The public site does not expose member listings, client records, agent messages or private directory entries.

## Network privacy and proof
The founder elected **not to display AAC member numbers publicly**. Do not put member totals in the hero, bottom marquee, testimonials, metadata, or other public content. Dated counts should remain in private business records rather than this public repository.
The separate AAC Facebook community has included thousands of agent participants, but the group is currently paused. Do NOT present Facebook participation as active website membership.
Retired website claims: "Thousands of agents" *on the app*, "More than 50 companies joined", "Proven Since 2016".
No public member photo grid or testimonials will be published until each individual explicitly consents to public marketing. Founder portrait and verified LinkedIn URL are pending.

## Membership and pricing
Membership is gated by license verification. Pricing is not stated on the live public site. Do not invent a free plan or price.

## Compliance
The prominent MLS/Clear Cooperation warning was removed from the hero at the founder's request. Keep the accurate explanation on /faq, /agent-rules, and the relevant informational page. Do not remove or soften the underlying obligation.

AAC is not an MLS and does not create an exception to local MLS, NAR Clear Cooperation or brokerage rules. Multi-brokerage listing promotion can constitute public marketing and may trigger MLS submission. Seller authorization by itself does not automatically exempt such promotion. Members are responsible for local requirements and seller instructions.

## SEO
Only public marketing and legal pages belong in the sitemap. Search answer retrieval must not expose member information; maintain existing AI-training restrictions unless separately authorized.
