# Why did changing a draft's status publish it?

## What the records show
234 Friend Street (L-1430) was created as a Draft at 12:51 PM ET. At 12:59 PM ET your account saved it from the app as Draft → Coming Soon. In AAC, every status except Draft counts as live, so that one save published the listing and Hot Sheet emails went out. After that it changed to Off Market (1:00 PM), then Cancelled (1:01 PM). Nothing changed the status by itself.
